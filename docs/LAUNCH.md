## Video asset

Use `release-assets/task-memory-cloud.mp4`, a 16:9 capture of an actual fresh Key Corridor solve, seed 23004, with the Connected ZHA reader and 64 active facts. Its live legend labels dormant, active, recovered and returned evidence. Recovered memories are included in active capacity.

## Reproduce the view

Run the local dashboard, choose the path-recovery study and Connected ZHA, then select **Memory cloud**. The view can also be selected with `memory_view=cloud` in the dashboard URL. The legend stays visible during fresh solves, and the chosen projection survives the new run.

Keep the source labels and the difference between originals/display samples visible when capturing. Do not describe active capacity as a total-memory limit: the archive, indexes, cache and connector graph are additional storage/work. The exact and graph controls match the measured connected-ZHA solve count.
